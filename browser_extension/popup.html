Privacy

Discovery sends only a site origin and hostname. Page measurements are separately opt-in and limited to monitored websites you authorize. They send the origin, JavaScript heap usage when available, and 30-second main-thread long-task counts and duration. Page paths, content, cookies, passwords, forms, and login tokens are not collected. These values are not total website RAM or CPU. Private browsing is disabled.
